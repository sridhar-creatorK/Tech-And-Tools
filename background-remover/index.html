Remove a photo background in your browser

Drop a photo and the WizOS Background Remover cuts out the subject and gives you a transparent PNG. The AI model runs on your device.

Open Background Remover

What you can do

  • Works with JPG, PNG and WebP photos
  • Transparent PNG download
  • Side-by-side preview of original and result
  • Your picture stays on your device

How it works

  1. Open the Background Remover and drop a photo.
  2. Wait while the model processes it. The first use downloads about 40 MB.
  3. Download the transparent PNG.

Questions

Is my photo uploaded?

No. The AI model is downloaded to your browser and the picture is processed there.

Why does the first use take longer?

The model is downloaded once, about 40 MB, and then it is much faster.

What format is the result?

A PNG with a transparent background.
