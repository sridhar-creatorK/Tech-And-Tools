Free PDF tools that run in your browser

WizOS Document Tools is a complete free PDF toolkit. Merge files, split pages, compress big PDFs, convert to and from Word, Excel, PowerPoint and images, and clean up documents. Your files are processed on your own device, so they are not uploaded to a server.

Open PDF Tools

What you can do

  • Merge PDFs, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, images, text and HTML files into one PDF, in the order you choose
  • Split a PDF into one file per page, or by ranges such as 1-3, 4-6
  • Remove pages, reverse the page order, or rotate pages
  • Compress a PDF with three levels: light, balanced and maximum
  • Add page numbers or a diagonal text watermark to every page
  • Convert PDF pages to PNG or JPG images, to PowerPoint slides, to plain text, or to Word
  • Read text from scanned PDFs and photos with OCR (English and many Indian and European languages)
  • Convert CSV to Excel (.xlsx) and each Excel sheet to CSV
  • Download many results at once as a single ZIP

How it works

  1. Open Document Tools and drop in your files.
  2. Choose what to create, such as Merge, Split, Compress or OCR.
  3. Set the pages or options if needed, press Create, then save the result.

Questions

Are my PDF files uploaded anywhere?

No. Merging, splitting, compressing and converting happen inside your browser. Only the optional OCR feature downloads language data the first time you use it.

Is there a file limit or a watermark?

WizOS adds no watermark and needs no account. Because the work happens in your browser, very large files can be slow; the tool stops at about 250 MB in total.

Can I compress a PDF?

Yes. Compression re-saves pages as images to shrink the file, so text stops being selectable. Choose Light for the best quality or Maximum for the smallest file.

Can it add a password to a PDF?

Not yet. WizOS can merge, split, reorder, number, watermark, compress and convert PDFs, but it does not encrypt them.
