What is WizOS?

WizOS is a free workspace of everyday tools by Tech Tool Wiz: PDF and document tools, image, video and audio converters, an AI assistant, a translator, a grammar checker, a letter writer, a screen recorder, a QR code generator and more.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no ads. Open a tool and start.

Are my files uploaded?

Most tools run entirely in your browser, so your files stay on your device. Tools that use AI or translation send only the text you submit to the service that answers.

Is WizOS free?

Yes. The AI tools have a fair daily limit so the free service stays available for everyone.
