The WizOS Media Converter changes the format of photos, video and audio without uploading them. Pick a format, adjust size or quality, and download the result.

Open Image, Video & Audio Converter

What you can do

  • Images to JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, ICO, PDF, GIF, TIFF, TGA or PPM
  • Video to MP4, WebM, MKV, MOV, AVI, OGV or animated GIF
  • Audio to MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A or FLAC, including audio extracted from video
  • Resize, crop, rotate and set quality for images
  • Trim video and choose an output height
  • Opens HEIC photos from iPhones and other cameras

How it works

  1. Open the Media Converter and add your files.
  2. Choose the output format and any size, crop, rotate or trim options.
  3. Press convert and save the new files.

Questions

Do my photos and videos leave my computer?

No. Conversion runs in your browser. The video engine is downloaded once from a public library host the first time you convert a video or a rare image format.

Which formats can I convert to?

Images: JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, ICO, PDF, GIF, TIFF, TGA and PPM. Video: MP4, WebM, MKV, MOV, AVI, OGV and GIF. Audio: MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A and FLAC.

Why is video conversion slow?

It runs on your own device, so speed depends on your computer and the file length. Trimming the clip or lowering the output height makes it faster.
