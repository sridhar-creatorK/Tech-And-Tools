The WizOS Screen Recorder captures your screen, a window or a browser tab straight from your browser. Add tab or system audio and your microphone, preview the result, and download it.

What you can do

  • Record the whole screen, a window or a browser tab
  • Include tab or system audio, and optionally your microphone
  • Preview before you save
  • No watermark, no time limit set by WizOS and no account
  • Recordings stay on your device

How it works

  1. Open the Screen Recorder and choose your audio options.
  2. Press Start recording and pick what to share.
  3. Press stop, preview the video and download it.

Questions

Does it add a watermark?

No. Recordings have no watermark and are not uploaded.

Which browsers work?

Chrome, Edge and Firefox on a computer. Phones and some browsers do not allow screen recording.

What file format do I get?

Usually WebM, or MP4 where your browser supports it. You can convert it with the WizOS Media Converter.
