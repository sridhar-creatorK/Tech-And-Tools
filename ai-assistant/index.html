Questions

Is the AI assistant really free?

Yes. There is no sign-up and no payment. A daily message limit keeps the free service available for everyone.

Can I trust the answers?

AI can make mistakes. Check anything important, such as legal, medical or financial information, with a reliable source.

What happens when the main AI is busy?

WizOS switches to a backup AI automatically and shows which one answered.
